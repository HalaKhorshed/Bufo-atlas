About this model

Proportions follow standard Bufo regularis practical dissections: ventral muscle sheets, viscera in situ, the urinogenital complex of both sexes, the ten pairs of spinal nerves, and the dorsal skull.

Built as a teaching schematic, not a specimen scan — use it to learn positions, relations and names, and confirm fine detail against your own dissection and your practical manual.
